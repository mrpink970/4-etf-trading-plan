## Video 2 — Problem 1: Perpetual Hoarding vs. The 15-Year Clock

**[Visual:** Green screen background showing your Section 2.4.1 Credit Percentage table.]

**[Camera Instruction:** Look directly at the lens. Point to the table behind you.]

**The Problem:** Because our system is realization-based, an ultra-wealthy investor can sit on a massive asset for thirty or forty years. Their unrealized gains compound by billions entirely untaxed, while everyday working people pay earned income tax on their paychecks every single year. At extreme scale, choosing never to sell becomes a permanent strategy to stay outside the tax system.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, look down at notes, tap record.]

**The Solution:** Section 2 of my blueprint introduces an annual deferral surcharge on fortunes over $20 million, managed by an asset-specific countdown clock. To be absolutely clear: you still pay your normal progressive capital gains tax whenever you finally sell an asset at a profit. This surcharge is simply an additional annual holding fee on paper growth that prices how long you defer that realization event.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, gesture cleanly to the 5-year line on the graphic.]

Here is exactly how the clock works: If you realize your gains and sell within the first 5 years, you receive a 100% tax credit for every dollar of the surcharge you paid, completely wiping it out so you only owe your standard capital gains tax.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, trace your finger down the declining percentages on the screen.]

But if you hold past Year 5, that surcharge credit decays by 10% every single year. By Year 15, your credit hits zero percent. At that point, the annual surcharge becomes a permanent, non-refundable cost on top of your final capital gains tax. We don't force asset sales—we simply ensure that keeping billions unrealized forever carries a permanent cost. Let's look at how the ultra-wealthy pull cash out of these hoarded assets next.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, lean forward slightly.]

Billionaires pledge their massive, untaxed growth as collateral for multi-million dollar bank lines of credit, pulling out tax-free cash for lifestyle consumption while keeping their gains permanently unrealized.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, tap the screen behind you showing Section 3.]

**The Solution:** Section 3 of my blueprint establishes Borrowing Neutrality. It leaves ordinary business and corporate financing completely unburdened. But the moment a covered household takes out personal, lifestyle debt exceeding $1 million or 10% of their asset's growth, the law triggers an automatic Deemed Realization Event.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, speak deliberately to the lens.]

The tax code treats that loan exactly like a sale—legally transforming those paper holdings into realized gains and forcing an immediate capital gains tax. It also resets the cost basis of the asset so there is no double taxation down the road. If you use debt to extract personal purchasing power from your appreciation, the system stops treating it as a loan and taxes it as a realization. ## Video 4 — Problem 3: Legal Fragmentation vs. Economic Look-Through

**[Visual:** Green screen background showing Section 4.4 text: *"Trust Look-Through Rules."*]

**[Camera Instruction:** Serious, analytical tone. Shrug your shoulders slightly.]

**The Problem:** If you try to pass any policy targeting unrealized gains, elite wealth managers immediately try to shield those assets from a realization event using legal fragmentation. They fracture fortunes across complex webs of shell companies, LLCs, and irrevocable trusts.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, look directly into the camera.]

They legally claim they no longer own the assets on paper, while secretly retaining full management control and using the trust to fund their lifestyles or pay personal expenses.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, gesture cleanly to the Section 4 text behind you.]

**The Solution:** Section 4 codifies a strict Economic Look-Through Rule. To be clear: this doesn't invalidate the trust system or collapse your legal firewalls. The trust remains fully intact. Instead, it simply allows the IRS to look through the structure solely for the purpose of this plan.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, speak deliberately.]

If a household retains veto power, directs administration, or derives personal financial benefit from an entity, those underlying assets and their accumulated unrealized gains are counted on the primary household ledger for tracking.

**[Camera Instruction:** Pause, lean forward.]

By allowing a look-through based on true economic reality rather than formal corporate labels, the framework ensures you can't use complex structures to escape the 15-year countdown clock. Now, let's wrap up and step back to look at the big picture.
